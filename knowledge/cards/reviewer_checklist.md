# Reviewer checklist

Used by the person approving a commentary draft. The draft is approved only when every item passes.

1. **Every number traces.** Every sentence containing a number cites a finding ID, and that finding exists for this run.
2. **Numbers match.** Each cited number equals the finding's `amount` (allowing for stated rounding).
3. **Every material bucket is handled.** Each bucket that meets the materiality rule has either a stated cause with evidence, or an explicit statement that no cause was identified.
4. **Basis change is fully reported.** Every register entry effective in the period is mentioned with its impact, including small or offsetting ones.
5. **Residual is reported as a residual.** It is stated with its amount and share of opening, marked within or above tolerance, and not attributed to a cause without evidence.
6. **The reconciliation holds.** Opening + explained movement + residual = closing, and the commentary says so.
7. **Nothing is in the commentary that is not in a finding.** No numbers, cells or causes appear that the findings do not support.

If any item fails, set the commentary status to `RETURNED` with the failing item noted, and re-run the investigation with a sharper question. Otherwise set `APPROVED`.
